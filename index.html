4 | Correlated Predictors: A Scientific Trap

Ridge

Often distributes weight across correlated predictors while shrinking both.

Lasso

Can retain one correlated predictor while driving another to zero. Which survives may depend on the sample.

Critical Thinking: If Lasso keeps X1 and removes X2, has it established that X1 matters scientifically and X2 does not?

5 | Why the Pipeline Matters

Scaling, imputation, feature selection, and PCA can learn from data. During cross-validation they must learn from each training fold, not the complete dataset.

✗ Leakage-Prone

All Data→Fit Selector→Cross-Validation

The validation observations already influenced feature selection.

✓ Leakage-Safer: Inside Each Fold

Training Fold→Fit Scaler→Fit Selector→Fit Model
Validation Fold→Transform Only→Predict→Score
fit()

Learn rules.

transform()

Apply learned rules.

fit_transform()

Learn and apply rules.

Professor's Challenge: On the validation fold, should the scaler use fit_transform() or transform()? What leaks if you choose incorrectly?
